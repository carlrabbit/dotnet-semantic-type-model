# Research

## Purpose

Research documents capture **active, non-authoritative investigation** that is useful before a specification, decision, or implementation direction is settled.

## Rules

- Research does not override specifications, architecture, decisions, or implemented behavior.
- Do not copy external project-setup, engineering, or methodology guides into this repository as research copies.
- When external material is provided for a task, extract only project-specific durable outcomes into the appropriate repository authority.
- When research reaches a durable conclusion, promote the conclusion into the relevant spec/decision/architecture document and remove stale research material when it no longer serves an active investigation.
- Do not use `docs/research/` as an archive.

## Current Research Index

No research documents are indexed by this reset.

If active research has been added after the reset baseline, preserve it only when it remains genuinely unresolved and non-authoritative.
